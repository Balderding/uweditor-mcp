The zones now close these squares for good, because a force field or a grating stands across
them (an object with a collision box as wide as a door's that cannot be stood on). What lies
behind in a dead end is reached with Telekinesis only. If one is passable, or a trigger takes
it away, say so.

## Ceilings: what the jump model does not know yet

The player is 23 height units tall (a floor step is 8) and the ceiling is at 128. The jump
model lets the feet rise as if nothing were overhead. In the game the rise stops under the
ceiling or under an object (`MOTION.C` `set_targz`: the target height is the underside less
the player's height), so the jump comes out lower and shorter than the model's.

Three kinds of place follow. The full lists are below; these are the ones to try first, one
of each kind:

| Kind | Game, level | From | To | What to see |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A plain jump from a high floor | UW1, 6 | (61, 40), height 13 | (61, 38) | does the jump still cross with the head at the ceiling? |
| The same | UW2, 44 | (8, 40), height 13 | (5, 40) | the same, a sprint jump |
| The jump spell under the ceiling, just | UW1, 1 | (45, 42), height 8 | (49, 42) | the head would pass the ceiling by 2 units |
| The jump spell under the ceiling, well over | UW1, 1 | (26, 25), height 11 | (26, 22) | the head would pass it by 13 units |
| A climb with the jump spell under a bridge | UW1, 1 | (23, 49), height 4 | (23, 48), height 8 | the bridge #878 is 2 steps over the head's path |
| A jump under a bridge | UW2, 3 | (20, 22), height 8 | (18, 22) | the bridge #596 is over the landing |
| A floor at height 14 or 15 | UW2, 25 | | (53, 16) | can the player stand there at all? The game refuses a step onto a floor whose top plus 23 is over 127 |
| The same | UW2, 65 | | (57, 55) | height 15 |

Most entries are jumps with the jump spell from a floor of height 8 or more: the spell lifts
the feet about 5.4 steps, so the head passes the ceiling from there. If the first rows show
that the ceiling cuts the jump as the source says, the model gets the ceiling and the lists
below need no further checking one by one.
